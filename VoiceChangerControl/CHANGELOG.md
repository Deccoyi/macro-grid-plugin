# Changelog — Voice Changer Control

New features and fixes in the Voice Changer Control plugin. For technical details, see [CHANGELOG-developer.md](CHANGELOG-developer.md).

## Unreleased
### New
- **Sound sources:** Play sound first asks for a source: Voicemod library, Community or My soundboards. Older buttons keep working.
- **All sounds in the search:** the sound picker now lists every sound of every soundboard, also your own and disabled ones.
- **Control the Voicemod app:** switch the voice changer, microphone mute, hear myself and background effect on and off, load a voice, pick a random voice, play sounds and stop all sounds.
- **Your own key:** the plugin needs your own Voicemod Control API key. It is not affiliated with Voicemod.
- **Refresh voice list:** reads the voice ids on request.
- **More variables:** voice name, hear myself, background effect, license, connection state, counts, last sound and last problem.
- **Pictures:** voices and sounds show their pictures in the search boxes and can be used as button icons.
- **Search boxes:** pick a voice or a sound by name from the lists of the Voicemod app.
- **Needs Macro Grid 1.6.0 or newer.**
